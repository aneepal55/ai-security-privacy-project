# Sleep basics

Most adults do best with 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night. Keeping a consistent sleep and wake time, even on weekends, helps regulate your body clock.

Limiting caffeine in the afternoon, keeping the bedroom cool and dark, and avoiding screens for an hour before bed can improve sleep quality.

# Hydration

Water needs vary by body size, activity, and climate. Pale yellow urine is a simple rough indicator of good hydration. Thirst, headaches, and dark urine can signal you need more fluids.
